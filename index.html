how this works

Five steps. None of them are “circle back.”

  1. Discovery call
    30 minutes. You describe what's broken or stuck. I tell you whether I'm actually the right person, or recommend someone who is.
  2. Written proposal
    A short doc: scope, deliverables, rate, engagement length. No deck. No discovery phase you have to pay for. If we're aligned, we sign.
  3. Embed & ship
    I join your Slack, your repos, your on-call where it makes sense. You get a senior engineer wired in, not an agency middleman.
  4. Async by default
    Written-first. I'll jump on a call when it's the right tool. Most weeks that's one or two. The rest is code, PRs, and Loom.
  5. Exit cleanly
    Engagements are time-boxed. By the end you have docs, a cleaner system, and the ability to run it without me. That's the whole point.
